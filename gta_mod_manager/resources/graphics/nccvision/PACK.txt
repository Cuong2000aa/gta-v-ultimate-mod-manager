NCCVision 3.1-ultimate — one flagship FPS-safe ReShade profile for GTA V

This pack ships with GTA V Ultimate Mod Manager. It does not redistribute
Rockstar assets. ReShade injector and community shaders keep their upstream
licenses (see THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt).

NCCVision follows the safe ReShade layer pattern:
  - Color / lighting only (no depth buffer effects)
  - No MXAO, DOF, SSR, MagicBloom, or ENB
  - One profile only: NCCVision Ultimate
  - Three passes: color-edge SMAA, NCCCinematic, AMD FidelityFX CAS

NCCVision Ultimate:
  - Soft filmic curve with teal shadows / warm highlights
  - Neighbor soft bloom + mild local contrast + vignette
  - Color-edge SMAA + restrained CAS for clarity without depth crashes
