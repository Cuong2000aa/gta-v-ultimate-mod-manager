CuongVision 3.0-ultimate — one flagship FPS-safe ReShade profile for GTA V

How premium packs (VisualV / NVE / QuantV) differ:
  - Core look = weather/timecycle inside mods/ (OpenIV), not ReShade alone
  - ReShade/ENB is only a thin color layer on top

CuongVision follows the safe ReShade layer pattern:
  - Injector: ReShade.asi when dinput8 ASI Loader exists; d3d11.dll fallback
  - No depth effects (no DOF / depth-bloom / MXAO)
  - One profile only: CuongVision Ultimate
  - Three passes: color-edge SMAA, CuongCinematic, AMD FidelityFX CAS

CuongVision Ultimate:
  - Color-edge SMAA smooths geometry, foliage, wire and road-edge aliasing
  - CuongCinematic adds rich color, highlight protection and restrained
    teal shadows / warm highlights without the pale FilmicPass transform
  - AMD FidelityFX CAS restores texture, road and grass clarity after AA
  - Does not read GTA V's depth buffer
  - Uses MIT-licensed SMAA/SweetFX assets and AMD's MIT-licensed CAS port

Limits:
  - Post-processing cannot replace low-resolution source textures or add grass objects
  - GTA V Anisotropic Filtering 16x is still recommended for distant road clarity
